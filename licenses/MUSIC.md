# Background music for 山静日长 · 地球 Online · 人生副本

## Track
- Filename: `assets/audio/daylight-walk.mp3` (also `daylight-walk.ogg`)
- Title: Daylight Walk
- Duration: 28-second seamless loop, mono, 22050 Hz
- Character: quiet daylight pad, slow walking pulse; written to sit under the game at low volume

## Provenance
This loop was synthesized for this project on 2026-11-28. It is an original procedural recording (sine pads, a soft footfall pulse, and filtered noise). It does not contain, sample, transcribe, or imitate any third-party soundtrack, including any Genshin Impact / HoYoverse / 米哈游 music, MIDI, or melody.

No third-party audio samples were used.
